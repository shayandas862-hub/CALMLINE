## 0. Is FCA regulation company-based or product-based? — BOTH
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=0 | aud=regulatory | type=overview | data=real*
UK financial regulation operates at two levels simultaneously: **firm-level (company-wide) obligations** on Aldercrest as an authorised entity (Part A), and **product-level conduct rules** on how each product is designed, disclosed, sold and serviced (Part B). Both apply at once: a firm can be compliant at company level yet breach product rules, and vice versa.

## PART A — FIRM-LEVEL / COMPANY-WIDE OBLIGATIONS

### A1. Authorisation under FSMA 2000
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A1 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Under **Part 4A FSMA 2000**, no firm may carry on a regulated activity (including "effecting or carrying out contracts of insurance") without permission. A life insurer is **dual-regulated**: authorised by the **PRA** (leading insurer authorisation) with **FCA** consent. Authorised firms appear on the FCA Financial Services Register.

### A2. Dual-regulation split (PRA prudential / FCA conduct)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A2 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**PRA** (Bank of England): prudential soundness — capital under **Solvency II / Solvency UK**, technical provisions, risk management, policyholder protection. **FCA:** conduct — customer treatment, disclosure, sales, complaints. Both must be satisfied continuously.

### A3. Threshold Conditions
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A3 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Schedule 6 FSMA 2000: legal status; location of offices; **effective supervision**; **appropriate resources**; **suitability**; **suitable business model**. Both regulators' Threshold Conditions apply to a dual-regulated insurer.

### A4. Principles for Businesses (PRIN)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A4 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
High-level Principles apply firm-wide. **Principle 11**: open and cooperative with regulators. **Principle 12 (Consumer Duty)**: "act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers."

### A5. The Consumer Duty (PRIN 2A)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A5 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Three layers: Principle 12; **cross-cutting obligations** — good faith (PRIN 2A.2.1R), avoid foreseeable harm (2A.2.8R), enable pursuit of financial objectives (2A.2.14R); **four outcomes** — products & services (2A.3), price & value (2A.4), consumer understanding (2A.5), consumer support (2A.6). In force 31 July 2023 (open products) / 31 July 2024 (closed). Annual **board-approved outcomes assessment** required. Guidance: **FG22/5**.

### A6. SM&CR (Senior Managers & Certification Regime)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A6 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Applied to insurers from December 2018. **Senior Managers Regime** (named SMFs, Statements of Responsibilities, Duty of Responsibility; insurer-specific SMFs incl. Chief Actuary, With-Profits Actuary); **Certification Regime** (annual fit-and-proper for significant-harm staff); **Conduct Rules** (incl. Rule 6 — deliver good outcomes). Handbook: SUP 10C, SYSC, COCON, FIT. **Reform note:** SM&CR changes phase in through 2026 (FCA **PS26/6**, PRA PS12/26) — monitor.

### A7. SYSC — systems and controls
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A7 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Effective governance, risk management, compliance and internal audit; **record-keeping (SYSC 9)** — orderly, sufficient, retrievable records; conflicts (A18); outsourcing (A15).

### A8. Financial promotions and the approver gateway
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A8 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**s.21 FSMA** restricts financial promotions; **COBS 4** requires clear, fair, not misleading communications (past-performance and risk-warning rules). **Approver gateway (real, from 7 February 2024):** a firm needs specific **FCA permission** to approve promotions for unauthorised persons (s.21 gateway) — Aldercrest must hold the permission before approving any third-party promotion.

### A9. Complaints handling (DISP) and the FOS
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A9 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Prompt acknowledgement; summary resolution communication if resolved by day 3 (DISP 1.5); **final response within 8 weeks** (DISP 1.6.2R) or a written explanation + FOS rights; **FOS referral within 6 months** of the final response (DISP 2.8.2R), subject to the 6-year/3-year limits. Reporting under **DISP 1.10** (currently half-yearly; see A9a); publication of a complaints summary where a return reports **500+ complaints** (DISP 1.10A, format DISP 1 Annex 1B R).

### A9a. Complaints reporting reform — PS25/19
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A9a | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
FCA **PS25/19** (3 December 2025) creates a **single consolidated complaints return** replacing five returns, moves to individual-firm reporting and fixed calendar half-years, and adds vulnerability data. First relevant reporting period: **1 January – 30 June 2027**. The 500-complaint publication threshold is retained.

### A10. FSCS
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A10 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
On firm failure: **long-term insurance** (life assurance; insured pensions/annuities) protected at **100% with no upper limit**. Context: most investments/SIPP operators £85,000; deposits **£120,000 from 1 December 2025** (PRA PS; temporary high balances £1.4m) — deposits are not Aldercrest's relevant limit.

### A11. Data protection — UK GDPR / DPA 2018 core
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A11 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Controller duties: lawful bases (Art 6), special-category conditions (Art 9 + DPA 2018 Sch 1), data-subject rights (access — one month; rectification; erasure subject to retention duties), security (Art 32), breach notification (**ICO ≤72h**, Art 33; individuals if high risk, Art 34). Supervisor: **ICO**. The **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025** amends the regime with phased commencement through 2025–26 — follow current ICO guidance.

### A12. Vulnerable customers — FG21/1 and the Equality Act
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A12 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**FG21/1**: understand vulnerability drivers (health, life events, resilience, capability); embed in design and service; skill staff; monitor outcomes — reinforced by the Consumer Duty. **Equality Act 2010 (real, complementary):** duty to make **reasonable adjustments** for disabled customers (formats, communication methods, process flexibility) — a legal duty distinct from, and alongside, FCA guidance.

### A13. Financial crime / AML
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A13 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Under **MLR 2017 (SI 2017/692)**, risk-based approach: **CDD** (regs 27–28; life-policy beneficiaries may be verified at/before payout); **SDD** where low-risk (reg 37 — "a life insurance policy for which the premium is low" is a low-risk factor; the old MLR 2007 numeric exemption (€1,000/€2,500) is repealed); **EDD** (regs 33, 35) for high-risk situations, **FATF-listed high-risk third countries** (SI 2024/69 reference), and **PEPs** (senior-management approval, source of wealth/funds, enhanced monitoring ≥12 months after leaving office; UK domestic PEPs = lower-risk starting point). Follow **JMLSG** guidance (Part II §7, life sector). Also: **POCA 2002** (SARs to the NCA via the MLRO; DAML 7-working-day notice; 31-day moratorium extendable to 186 days; **tipping-off** offence s.333A) and **OFSI** financial sanctions (strict liability; freeze and report).

### A14. Operational resilience (PS21/3)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A14 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
FCA/PRA operational-resilience rules (FCA **PS21/3**; in force 31 March 2022): identify **important business services**, set **impact tolerances**, map and test; firms had to be able to **remain within impact tolerances by 31 March 2025** — now a continuing obligation. For Aldercrest, claims payment, policy servicing and contact channels are candidate IBSs; the AI system's availability falls inside this framework.

### A15. Outsourcing and third parties (SYSC 8 / PRA SS2/21)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A15 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**SYSC 8**: outsourcing critical functions must not materially impair controls, and the firm **retains full responsibility**; due diligence, written agreements, access/audit and exit plans required. PRA **SS2/21** sets outsourcing/third-party expectations for insurers. Applies squarely to any vendor-supplied AI/RAG platform: Aldercrest remains accountable for its outputs.

### A16. PECR — electronic marketing
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A16 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
The **Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003** sit alongside UK GDPR: electronic marketing (email/SMS/calls) generally needs **consent**; the **soft opt-in** allows marketing to existing customers for similar products with an opt-out at collection and in every message; ICO enforces (fines). Marketing-consent servicing (product docs §II.6.4) implements this.

### A17. UK GDPR operational duties — RoPA, DPIA, DPO, transfers
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A17 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**Records of Processing Activities** (Art 30) must be maintained. **Data Protection Impact Assessments** (Art 35) are required for high-risk processing — **innovative technology/AI profiling of customers squarely triggers a DPIA**: Aldercrest must DPIA the RAG assistant before deployment. A **DPO** (Art 37) is appointed given large-scale processing of special-category data. **International transfers** (Art 44+) need adequacy or safeguards (**IDTA / UK Addendum to SCCs**) — relevant if any AI/vendor processing occurs outside the UK.

### A18. Conflicts, whistleblowing, training & competence
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A18 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**SYSC 10**: identify and manage conflicts of interest (e.g. sales incentives vs suitability). **SYSC 18**: whistleblowing arrangements, a whistleblowers' champion, protected channels. **TC sourcebook**: staff advising/servicing retail business must be and remain competent (qualifications, CPD, supervision).

### A19. Data flows to authorities — the crime-and-taxation exemption
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=A19 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**DPA 2018 Sch 2 para 2** permits setting aside certain transparency/rights provisions **only to the extent** compliance would prejudice crime prevention/detection or tax assessment/collection — case-by-case, minimum necessary; court orders compel disclosure; tipping-off restrictions (A13) override customer-facing explanations. Operationalised in each product doc §II.10.

## PART B — PRODUCT-SPECIFIC CONDUCT RULES (mapped to the three products)

### B1. Which conduct sourcebook — COBS vs ICOBS?
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B1 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**COBS**: designated investment business — **investment bonds, personal pensions**, and life policies with an investment/surrender element (unit-linked whole of life). **ICOBS**: non-investment (pure) protection — a whole of life plan with no surrender value (firms may elect COBS). Practical test: surrender/unit value → COBS; none → ICOBS.

### B2. PROD 4 — product governance
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B2 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Documented product approval; defined **target market**; **fair value** assessment; appropriate distribution; regular review — all three products; dovetails with Consumer Duty outcomes 1–2.

### B3. Disclosure — KID / Key Features and the CCI transition
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B3 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Investment bonds (insurance-based investment products): **KID** under PRIIPs, transitioning to the **Consumer Composite Investments (CCI)** regime (FCA **PS25/20**) — track implementation. Pensions and life policies: **Key Features Document** + illustration (COBS 13/14).

### B4. Suitability and appropriateness
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B4 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Advised sales: **COBS 9A** suitability. Non-advised: **COBS 10A** appropriateness.

### B5. Cancellation rights (COBS 15)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B5 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**30 calendar days** for life policies and pension contracts (incl. transfers); 14 days for certain other contracts; ICOBS pure protection also 30 days. A **market-loss (shortfall) adjustment** may apply to single-premium investment refunds. **PCLS/UFPLS are not cancellable contracts** — tax consequences irreversible (FCA/HMRC statement, 2025).

### B6. Pension-specific rules (COBS 19)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B6 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
**Transfers/conversions** (safeguarded/DB ≥ £30,000 → regulated advice mandatory); **retirement risk warnings** and the **Pension Wise "nudge"** (COBS 19.7); IGCs and the 0.75% default charge cap for qualifying workplace schemes (COBS 19.5/19.6).

### B6a. Investment pathways (COBS 19.10)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B6a | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Non-advised consumers entering drawdown must be offered **four investment pathways** (no plans 5yr / annuity 5yr / long-term income 5yr / take all 5yr), with cash-holding warnings — implemented in Doc 3 §9.2 and monitored in ops MI.

### B7. With-profits (COBS 20)
*meta: doc=04-FCA | sec=B7 | aud=regulatory | type=legal | data=real*
Fair treatment of with-profits policyholders; **With-Profits Actuary**; published **PPFM**; governs bonuses/MVR practice (Doc 2 §3.6).
